Odoo 19 ERP Implementation Portfolio
An end-to-end ERP system implementation and workflow optimization prototype designed on **Odoo 19** to streamline business operations across cross-functional modules.
🛠️ Implemented Modules & Workflows
1. Sales & Manufacturing to Accounting Flow
Complete Order-to-Cash (O2C) workflow starting from eCommerce/Sales quotes down to automated invoice generation and journal entries.
Full integration with Manufacturing (BoM) to auto-trigger production based on confirmed sales.
2. Inventory & Multi-Warehouse Management
* Advanced multi-warehouse configurations and automated replenishment (Reordering Rules).
* Product data architecture managed and imported via bulk Excel frameworks for clean data mapping.
3. Purchase & Procurement Module
* Procure-to-Pay (P2P) automation linked directly to low-stock warehouse thresholds and vendor management.
4. HR & Advanced Payroll Management
* Employee contract structures, dynamic salary rules, and automated payroll journal creation.
5. Repairs & Field Service Module
* End-to-end tracking for post-sale maintenance, service logs, and replacement part inventory deductions.
6. Odoo Automations & Server Actions
Custom automated rules, field mapping, and Python-driven validation scripts (e.g., dynamic sales discount enforcement and live UX notifications).
## 🚀 Technical Highlights
*Version:Odoo 19 (Community/Enterprise testing environment).
* Data Migration: Bulk Excel importing templates for catalogs and inventory structures.
* Customization: Studio customization, server actions, and advanced Python validation logic.
